How I work

Analyst relations is about influencing the influencers.

My goal is simple: build meaningful, lasting relationships with the analysts who cover the B2B markets I work in. Four ideas shape how I run a program.

Relationships come before reports
Analysts form views on a company all year, from many sources, long before any briefing or evaluation. So I aim for steady, two-way dialogue rather than campaigns timed to a research cycle.
Ask before you announce
The best analyst conversations happen while strategy and product direction are still open. I bring analysts in for perspective on where the market is going instead of unveiling a finished story and hoping for applause.
Right analysts, not all analysts
Analysts play different roles, and not every one moves buying decisions in your category. I rank them by role and relevance to buyers so time goes where it counts.
Executive backing, insight flowing inward
A program works when leadership shows up for it. And the value comes back into the company as messaging, competitive intelligence, and guidance for product and sales.
